Adaptations: blank outer space trimmed via the SVG viewBox; ink set to light grey for the dark interface. The Google G reuses the existing multicolour vector glyph, uniformly scaled beside the original Pay lettering. No logo text is typeset from an app font. SVG path geometry is retained for the wordmarks.
Revision 11 intro tile treatment:
Apple Pay retains the existing full vector path, in black on a white tile.
Google Pay retains the existing multicolour G and full Pay path; Pay uses #5f6368 on white.
Visa is the user's unmodified Visa_Inc._logo_(2021–present).svg.webp, copied byte-for-byte.
Mastercard embeds the user's unmodified Mastercard_logo.webp pixels in an SVG viewport that crops only surrounding white space and preserves the TM mark.
All four tile containers have equal dimensions. Logo aspect ratios are preserved.
The watermarked stock preview is used only as a tile-style reference.
